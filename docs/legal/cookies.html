Cookies Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-25

We use no cookies

Neither the Pokered Save Editor 2 desktop application nor this documentation website sets any cookies, and neither uses any third-party tracking.

Local storage (this website)

The documentation site stores a few values in your browser's local storage. Local storage is not a cookie: it stays on your device and is never sent to a server. What is kept:

  • Reader preferences — your theme, accent, and text-size choices.
  • Fairy Fox coins — a local coin balance and the record of which pages you have opened today (for the cosmetic reading counter; no monetary value).

You can clear all of it at any time using the reader panel's Reset control, the coins panel's Clear my data link, or by clearing site data in your browser. The desktop application stores nothing in a browser at all.
